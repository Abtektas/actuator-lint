# SDF012: a motor gets no speed commands

Level: error. Runs with `--sdf`.

## What it checks

Each `MulticopterMotorModel` plugin:

- `commandSubTopic` is `command/motor_speed`, the topic PX4 publishes the
  motor speeds on (`GZMixingInterfaceESC.cpp`);
- no `robotNamespace` moves the topic away from the model name;
- the link and joint it names exist;
- the `motorNumber` values of all plugins are 0 to N-1, each once.

## Why it matters

A motor plugin that listens on another topic, or under another name, never
receives a command. With `command/motor_speeds` in the four plugins of PX4's
x500, PX4 armed, the motors stayed still and the vehicle did not lift in
SITL. A repeated or missing `motorNumber` leaves a motor without a command or
gives two motors the same one.

## What to do

Use the topic `command/motor_speed`, remove `robotNamespace`, and number the
motors 0 to N-1 in the order of the `SIM_GZ_EC_FUNCn` outputs.
