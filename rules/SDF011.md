# SDF011: a rotor joint cannot reach full speed

Level: error. Runs with `--sdf`.

## What it checks

The joint named by each `MulticopterMotorModel` plugin:

- its type is neither `revolute` nor `continuous`, so it cannot turn;
- it is `revolute` with position limits less than one turn (2π) apart;
- it has a `<velocity>` limit below `maxRotVelocity` /
  `rotorVelocitySlowdownSim`, the joint speed the motor model asks for at full
  command (`rotorVelocitySlowdownSim` is 10 when it is not set).

## Why it matters

The motor model computes thrust from the joint speed. A joint that cannot turn
far or fast enough gives far less thrust than the plugin parameters promise.
With a velocity limit of 20 rad/s on the rotor joints of PX4's x500 (the motor
model needs 100 rad/s), the vehicle armed and did not leave the ground in
SITL, the symptom of [PX4 forum thread 45612](https://discuss.px4.io/t/45612).

A `rotorVelocitySlowdownSim` of 1 is not reported: the thrust does not depend
on it, and x500 flew normally with it.

## What to do

Use a `revolute` joint with limits of -1e16 and 1e16, as PX4's models do, and
remove the velocity limit or set it above the value the finding names.
