# SDF020: the motors of the model do not match the control allocation rotors

Level: error, or warning for a layout that differs in shape. Runs with `--sdf`
when the parameter file sets `CA_ROTOR_COUNT`.

## What it checks

PX4's gz airframes connect output n (`SIM_GZ_EC_FUNCn` = 100 + k) to control
allocation rotor k-1 and to the motor plugin with `motorNumber` n-1. For each
motor plugin, through that chain:

- error: the number of motor plugins differs from `CA_ROTOR_COUNT`;
- error: the rotor sits on the other side, front or back or left or right,
  than its `CA_ROTORn_PX`, `CA_ROTORn_PY` (FRD, so the y of the model, which is
  FLU, changes sign);
- error: its `turningDirection` does not match the sign of `CA_ROTORn_KM`
  (positive for `ccw`);
- warning: its position differs from the control allocation layout by more
  than 30% of the arm, once the model is scaled to the size of the layout.

## Why it matters

When a motor of the model is not where control allocation thinks it is, PX4
sends the roll and pitch commands to the wrong motors and the vehicle flips on
takeoff. With motorNumber 0 and 1 of PX4's x500 swapped, it flipped in SITL.
[PX4 forum thread 48713](https://discuss.px4.io/t/48713) shows such a flip with
a motor set-up copied from another model; that thread used Gazebo Classic,
which this rule does not read, and its tuning is not something a static check
can judge.

Control allocation normalises the effectiveness of a multicopter, so the size
of the layout does not matter, only its shape. PX4's own models differ in
shape by up to 25% (x500 in v1.17.0, standard_vtol and tiltrotor on main in
November 2026) and fly, hence the 30%.

## What to do

Make `motorNumber`, `SIM_GZ_EC_FUNCn` and `CA_ROTORn_*` describe the same
motor, and the turning direction match `CA_ROTORn_KM`.
