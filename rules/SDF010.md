# SDF010: a rotor pushes along another axis than the one it spins about

Level: error. Runs with `--sdf`.

## What it checks

For each `MulticopterMotorModel` plugin, the z axis of its rotor link and the
axis of its rotor joint, both in the model frame. The rule reports a rotor
whose two axes are not parallel (|cos| below 0.99).

## Why it matters

The gz-sim motor model (`MulticopterMotorModel.cc`) applies the thrust along
the z axis of the rotor **link**, while the rotor turns about the **joint**
axis. When a rotor link is rotated, for example by a mesh export, and the
joint axis is kept upright with `expressed_in="__model__"`, the propeller
visibly spins but pushes sideways. With the four rotor links of PX4's x500
rolled by 90° in this way, the vehicle armed, the propellers turned, and it did
not leave the ground in SITL. That is the symptom of
[PX4 forum thread 45612](https://discuss.px4.io/t/45612), where the cause was
not found; this rule and [SDF011](SDF011.md) and [SDF013](SDF013.md) cover
the causes that give it.

## What to do

Give the rotor link the orientation of the thrust (z up for a lift rotor) and
put any rotation of the mesh into the `<visual>` pose, or express the joint
axis in the link frame.
