# SDF013: the rotors cannot lift the model

Level: error at a thrust to weight ratio of 1 or less, warning below 1.3. Runs with `--sdf`.

## What it checks

The upward thrust of all rotors at full speed against the weight of the model:
`motorConstant` × speed² for each motor plugin, with the speed limited by
`maxRotVelocity` and by `SIM_GZ_EC_MAXn` of its output, times the upward part
of its thrust axis; the weight is the mass of all links times 9.81 m/s². A link
without `<inertial>` counts 1 kg, the SDFormat default.

The rule runs for models with at least one rotor that points mostly up, so not
for fixed-wing aircraft. It is not checked when a motor plugin names a link or
joint the model does not have ([SDF012](SDF012.md)).

## Why it matters

The motor model gives `motorConstant` × speed² of thrust at most, whatever
`rotorVelocitySlowdownSim` is. A model that is too heavy for its rotors does
not lift. With the base link of PX4's x500 made 4.5 kg instead of 2 kg (thrust
to weight 0.76), the vehicle armed and stayed on the ground in SITL. Below 1.3
there is little thrust left for control. PX4's own multicopter models have
1.41 (x500_gimbal) or more.

## What to do

Check the masses of the links and the `motorConstant` and `maxRotVelocity` of
the motors against the real vehicle, and the `SIM_GZ_EC_MAXn` values.
